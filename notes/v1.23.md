## LocalPaint v1.23

Models with several materials now work per material instead of being treated as one.

- Importing an FBX (or GLB) ignores the textures the file points to. LocalPaint reads the meshes, the UVs and which material each part uses, with no missing-texture errors and no slow-down from big embedded textures.
- Every part keeps the material it's linked to. Materials with the same name count as one across all meshes, like Substance's texture sets.
- Baking: the ID map now gives each material its own colour by default. Parts that share a material share the colour, even on separate meshes, and the high poly's colours match the low poly's by material name. Before, it fell back to one colour per object and the materials were lost.
- Dropping an asset on the model fills the material of the part you drop it on when the model has more than one material. Change it under Drop onto.
- The polygon fill (P) has a new Material mode.
