## LocalPaint v1.27

- GPU baking no longer causes lag spikes. The ray tracing is split into small slices, one per frame, and LocalPaint never freezes the window waiting for the graphics card, so the app stays smooth while it bakes.
- When LocalPaint is in the background or minimised, it bakes in big pieces at full speed.
- Reading the baked results back from the graphics card no longer stalls the window.
- Switching texture sets, including while it bakes all of them, no longer re-checks the UVs every time.
- The baked maps are the same as before.
