## LocalPaint v1.29

Projecting pictures onto the model now works like Substance Painter.

- Drop a picture on the model and pick which channel it goes into: Base color, Height, Metallic, Normal, Roughness or Emissive.
- A dashed box shows where it lands:
  - Drag inside the box, or the middle square, to move it.
  - Drag a green corner to resize; the opposite corner stays put. Hold Shift to keep the shape.
  - Drag a green edge handle to stretch one side.
  - Drag the red or green arrow to slide it that way only. Drag the square end of an arrow to stretch that way.
  - Drag the round handle above the box to turn it. Hold Shift for 15° steps.
- The picture follows the cursor exactly while you move and resize it.
- Clicking the model while placing a picture no longer makes it jump.
- The top bar has Face camera, Reset and Done.
- Transparent pictures now also cut out when they're projected into Height, Roughness or another single channel.
